# Steam release gates

Target: premium PC release on Windows, macOS and Linux. Current source and developmentplayers are not a release approval.

- Three campaign chapters, victory/defeat/retry, progress reset confirmation and isolated local saves.
- Main/settings/controls/credits/story/LAN menus, keyboard/mouse/gamepad navigation and gameplay bindings; audio/display preferences and rebinding.
- Actual physical Xbox/PlayStation/SteamInput and keyboard playthroughs, disconnect/reconnect and controller-only LANflow are required before advertising full controller support.
- Multi-process native LAN replication is separate from two-machine LAN validation, router/firewall behavior and multiplayer balance.
- Finish art/animation, encounter pacing, collision/navigation, accessibility, audio mix and performance budgets on target hardware.
- Verify entitlement and commercial distribution terms for each actual selected purchase. Never publish raw licensed payloads or package source.
- Build and test on Windows/Linux native hosts, sign/notarize distribution packages as appropriate, and validate startup stability.
- Steamworks partner setup, real AppID/depots, store art/trailer/descriptions, SDK/SteamInput integration ifchosen, build/store review and releasechecklists remain owner/account-specific gates. Do notinvent AppIDs or readinesscheckmarks.

Primary requirements: https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/releasing and https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/features/steam_controller/getting_started_for_players.
